Data from Open Food Facts, a crowdsourced open database — coverage and accuracy vary by product. NOVA groups classify processing method, not healthfulness — many NOVA 4 foods (e.g. mycoprotein products) show neutral or positive health markers in trials despite being ultra-processed by definition. The "UPF concern flag" checks for a short list of additives with published mechanistic or trial evidence of a specific effect (mainly gut-microbiome disruption) — it deliberately does NOT count E-numbers generally, since most additives (e.g. E263, E509) are metabolically inert at food-additive doses and an E-number count is a poor proxy for risk. This is a personal heuristic, not a validated safety rating or medical advice.
